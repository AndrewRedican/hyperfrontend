# Notice

The linter's code is written for this workspace. Its rules' specifications are not all original: many of the cases in the `*.spec.ts` files beside the rules under `src/rules/` were adapted from the test suites of the projects whose rules this linter reimplements, and the messages, options and fixes those rules answer with follow the same projects. Each family's cases come from:

- ESLint (MIT, OpenJS Foundation and contributors): the `javascript` family
- typescript-eslint (MIT, typescript-eslint and contributors): the `typescript` family
- eslint-plugin-jsdoc (BSD-3-Clause, Gajus Kuizinas): the `jsdoc` family
- eslint-plugin-react (MIT, Yannick Croissant): the `react` family
- eslint-plugin-react-hooks (MIT, Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates): the `react-hooks` family
- eslint-plugin-package-json (MIT, Josh Goldberg and contributors): the `package-json` family
- Nx (MIT, Narwhal Technologies Inc.): the `projects` family

The `workspace` family's cases were written for this workspace. The licence texts are kept under `licenses/` beside this file.
