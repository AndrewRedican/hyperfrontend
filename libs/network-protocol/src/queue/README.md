# queue

FIFO processing queues for the seal and open pipeline stages that connect senders, receivers, and channels.

[`createQueue`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-createQueue) wraps a [`MessageHandler`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-MessageHandler) callback into a typed `Queue<T>` that processes messages strictly one at a time in arrival order, with [`stop`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-Queue-prop-stop), [`resume`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-Queue-prop-resume), [`size`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-Queue-prop-size), [`isRunning`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-Queue-prop-isRunning), and [`currentMessage`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-Queue-prop-currentMessage). [`createSealQueue`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-createSealQueue) and [`createOpenQueue`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-createOpenQueue) (the [`SealQueueCreater`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-SealQueueCreater) and [`OpenQueueCreater`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-OpenQueueCreater) shapes) bind a session's [`PacketSealer`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/#api-PacketSealer) or [`PacketOpener`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/#api-PacketOpener) to a queue: plaintext packets in and sealed frames out, or frames in and plaintext packets out. Each rejected input is reported through a [`QueueFailureHandler`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-QueueFailureHandler) with the reason and the thrown error, and the queue moves on. [`QueueCreatorArguments`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-QueueCreatorArguments) and [`QueueCreatorValidity`](https://www.hyperfrontend.dev/docs/libraries/network-protocol/queue/#api-QueueCreatorValidity) cover the argument validation, so a misconfigured stage fails at construction rather than mid-stream.
